The Problem

Employees want to grow - but do not know what to focus on.

Modern employees are not lacking ambition. They are lacking clarity.

i

Information overload

Career advice, AI content, internal goals, and learning links arrive without prioritization.

?

Unclear direction

Employees can see role titles, but not the practical growth path between them.

f

Fragmented feedback

Feedback, learning, calendar work, and goals live in separate places.

p

Invisible progress

Small improvements happen in work, but they rarely become visible or motivating.

E

Meet Eira

The Quiet Explorer

"I do not need more motivation. I need clarity about what truly matters."
Target User
27 years old Product Marketing Executive Hybrid office worker Mobile-first during the day Consumes career, AI, productivity, and business content often

Pain points

Overwhelmed by content Unsure which skill to prioritize Compares herself silently Wants to know if she is growing in the right direction
Key Insight

Career Path needs two axes.

Career Path is not only about role progression. It is also about capability maturity.

Axis 1

Career Direction

Where employees can go next.

Current role Target role Future role
Axis 2

Capability Growth

What employees need to build.

Communication Critical thinking Strategic thinking Leadership AI collaboration
